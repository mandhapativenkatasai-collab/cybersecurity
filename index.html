Cyber security

Cyber security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, programs, and data from digital attacks, damage, or unauthorized access. It focuses on three core rules: keeping data secret, keeping data safe from changes, and making sure systems are ready to use.

Phishing

Phishing is a a cyberattack where scammers trick people into sharing private information, like passwords or credit card numbers, by pretending to be a trusted company.

Ransomware

Ransomware is a type of malware attack in which the attacker locks and encrypts the victim’s data, important files and then demands a payment to unlock and decrypt the data.
